Grafito

A window into your Linux machine

$ grafito --port 3000
Grafito started as a log viewer. It grew into a window into your Linux machine: read the journal, watch services and processes, steer your containers, install apps — your whole system, live, in one self-hosted web page.

modules 02–07 — the whole machine

02The Journal, Live

Every line your machine writes, as it writes it. Filter by text, unit, tag, time range or priority, watch new entries stream in live, and click the frequency chart to jump straight to any moment.

03AI-Powered Log Analysis

Stuck on a confusing error? Get intelligent explanations from LLMs with a single click, and ask follow-up questions in context. Works with Anthropic, OpenAI, Groq, Together, any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (local Ollama included) and more — completely optional!

04Server Dashboard

The state of your machine at a glance: system health, the state of your systemd units, and a history chart of memory, disk and load — sampled in the background, stored as plain JSONL, no database. Optional Gotify push alerts for failing units and full disks.

05Process Monitor

htop on a page: per-core CPU meters and a live process table that sorts by any column and filters by user, pid or command. Click a row for full /proc details and a jump into its logs.

06Containers & Apps

Your compose stacks and their health at a glance: start, stop, restart and update them with job output streaming into the page, Dockge-style. Then install popular self-hosted apps without writing YAML via the Runtipi-compatible app store — pick a port, fill in the form, and Grafito pulls and runs it.

07Self-Hosted & Lightweight

Everything stays on your hardware: Grafito runs on your own server as one small, self-contained binary. Built with Crystal and HTMX, it's fast and easy on resources.
